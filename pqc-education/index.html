Post-Quantum Cryptography

Preparing your data for the quantum computing era

What is PQC?

Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) is a family of cryptographic algorithms designed to resist attacks from both classical and quantum computers. As quantum computers grow in power, current encryption (RSA, ECDSA) may become vulnerable.

Why Now?

NIST finalized its first set of PQC standards in 2024. Organizations worldwide are beginning the transition. The "harvest now, decrypt later" threat means your data encrypted today could be cracked by future quantum computers.

Skand & PQC

Skand Browser uses AES-256-GCM encryption for local data storage. While not a full PQC implementation, this provides quantum-resistant symmetric encryption for your app data, API keys, and sync payloads.

Migration Path

The transition to PQC is gradual. Hybrid approaches (combining classical + PQC algorithms) are recommended. Skand will adopt NIST-approved algorithms as browser engine support matures.
